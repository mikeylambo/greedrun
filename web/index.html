The Job

How to Play

Move
Drag anywhere to steer Jo — press and hold, and he follows your thumb. (Keyboard: WASD or arrows.)
Grab
Walk over loot to pocket it. Everything you carry is Greed Load — it makes you richer, but slower, louder, more marked, and more hunted.
Drop
Overloaded? Press Q (or tap DROP) to shed your heaviest piece — it cools your greed, quiets your bag, and can talk a sealed shutter back open.
Watch
Guards chase what they see and give up when they lose you. Swiveling sentries can't see through walls — use cover, read the sweep.
Climb
Walk up a staircase to reach high ground — guards can’t follow you up. Step off a ledge to drop fast, but a loaded landing is loud and can cost a heart.
Escape
The glowing exit is where you started. Hold it to leave and bank your haul. Get caught and the vault keeps everything.
Heat
The more you carry, the hotter it gets — guards grow faster and sharper-eyed, and at high Heat a Bounty Hunter enters. But danger pays: a hotter run sells for more.
Decide
Glowing artifacts stop you for a choice: take it whole for gold, pry it for clean scraps, or spend it for power — a boon that bends the run.
Places
Locations fight differently: flood water slows but silences you, wind shoves everyone, the dark hides all but your lantern, and the Mint’s presses crush whatever lingers.
Tools
The Workbench sells tools; one rides per job, limited charges. Tap TOOL (or Space) — smoke, decoy, grapple, lockpicks, a portable portal. Never required; always greedy.
Word
Escapes earn a name — earn it twice and your kind of work pays +25%, one buyer knows you. The Gilded Fake: rich in the bag, pennies at the fence, fine bait dropped.
Notoriety
Selling hot goods to the Syndicate raises your Notoriety. High Notoriety makes every job start hotter and the Hunter come sooner. Returning goods cools it back down.
Secrets
Strongboxes crack open if you stand close — loudly. A shimmer in the floor marks a hidden stash. A lost thief’s tool crate swaps your kit for the night.
Ways out
At Heat T4 the east wall breaches — a second exit, no temptation attached. The drop chute banks your haul at 70¢ on the dollar, yours even if you fall. The altar by the door offers one gift per night. Old trapdoor tunnels drop you back near the door — once, loudly. Flooded rooms are slow but drown your clinking; a brass key (or a lockpick) opens the locked shutter.
Temptation
Reach the exit rich and it whispers one more thing — a Vault Heart, deeper in. How greedy can you be before the vault owns you?
